This little 3x3 cube has **43 quintillion** ways it could be solved and most of us don't even know 1 solution, but using basic problem solving most of us can solve alteast one side.

We all think this as a children’s toy but it was created by Ernő Rubik as a **3D teaching tool to help explain spatial relationships and geometry** in 1974 and called it Magic Cube

**Ironically, Rubik had invented a puzzle that he couldn’t solve himself.** but now people solve it in seconds.

Not bad for something that was never supposed to be a toy.
